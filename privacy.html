Privacy Policy

Quick Panel: Themes & Controls • Effective Date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the data practices of Quick Panel: Themes & Controls ("Quick Panel", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by hamza arbi, for the Android application (com.ns.swipepanel.theme.iconwidgets).

1. Application Purpose

Quick Panel is an Android customization application providing swipe-gesture control panel overlays, live wallpaper customization, theme presets, and desktop widgets.

2. Accessibility Service Scope

Quick Panel includes an Accessibility Service (the app’s Accessibility Service) used solely to perform the following functions:

  • To open the Control Center panel overlay from the screen edge and show it on top of other applications and the home screen.
  • When you tap Screenshot in the Control Center panel, to take a screenshot on your behalf using system accessibility actions.
  • Optional: if you turn on "Keep Control Center always active", Quick Panel shows a persistent notification so the phone keeps the service running. You can turn it off at any time.

Accessibility Scope & Non-Collection:

  • the app’s Accessibility Service does NOT read, inspect, or store your screen content or what you type.
  • No accessibility event data is stored, logged, or transmitted off your device.
  • Accessibility service access is completely optional and can be turned off at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility.

3. Media Session Access for Music Controls

When the optional Notification Access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE) permission is granted by the user, Quick Panel accesses Android's MediaSessionManager to discover active media sessions for the control panel music card. This allows displaying the active song title, artist name, play/pause status, and album art, and invoking playback transport controls (play, pause, next, previous). This media session information is processed purely in local device memory and is never logged, saved, or transmitted to any server.

4. On-Device System Permissions

Quick Panel requests the following optional permissions strictly to perform user-requested features. These permissions do not block the rest of Quick Panel if denied:

  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Allows displaying the control panel overlay over other apps.
  • Write System Settings (WRITE_SETTINGS): Allows adjusting display brightness via the control panel brightness slider or toggling auto-brightness.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): Displays Bluetooth connection status and opens Bluetooth settings.
  • Wi-Fi State (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): Displays Wi-Fi status and opens Wi-Fi settings.
  • Notification Access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE): Discovers active media sessions for local music playback controls.
  • Do Not Disturb Access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY): Turns Do Not Disturb on or off from the control panel. Quick Panel does not read or change your notification content.

5. Local App Discovery for Shortcuts

To support customizing launcher app shortcuts in the Themes section, Quick Panel queries installed applications locally on your device. No list of installed applications is transmitted or uploaded to any external server.

6. Asset Downloads & Network Requests

Quick Panel fetches public theme catalogs, wallpaper previews, and video assets from public Supabase CDN storage endpoints over HTTPS. When making network requests to download assets, standard network request information (such as your IP address, requested file URL, and timestamp) is received by Supabase infrastructure to deliver the requested files.

7. Analytics & Diagnostics

Quick Panel integrates Google Firebase SDKs to maintain performance and fix crashes:

  • Firebase Analytics: Firebase Analytics collects app usage events, such as screen views and feature interactions, together with app-instance identifiers. This information is used to produce usage reports and improve Quick Panel. Advertising ID collection is explicitly disabled in Quick Panel's configuration and manifest.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Receives stack traces, device model information, Android OS version, and diagnostic crash identifiers when an unexpected error occurs.

For details on Google Firebase data processing, visit the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy Guidelines.

8. Feedback & Support Submissions

When you use the in-app Feedback feature, feedback is prepared using Android's system share sheet and sent using an email or messaging app selected by you. The shared message includes your selected problem category, feedback text, device hardware/OS details, and any screenshot you choose to attach. No feedback is sent automatically without your explicit action.

9. Data Retention, Storage & Deletion

  • Local Application Data: Clearing app data or uninstalling Quick Panel removes app-private data from that installation. It does not automatically delete provider-held records, Android backups, or files you have separately saved or shared.
  • Android Cloud Backups: Quick Panel does not allow Android cloud backup of app data (allowBackup is disabled), so your app preferences are not included in Google cloud backups.
  • Google Analytics Data Retention: In Google Analytics, event-level data is retained for 2 months, and user-level data (associated with app-instance identifiers) is retained for 14 months. Reset on new user activity is enabled, meaning new activity from a user resets the retention clock for their user-level identifier. These retention settings apply to user-level and event-level data and do not affect standard aggregated summary reports.
  • Firebase Crashlytics Data Retention: Firebase Crashlytics retains crash stack traces and associated diagnostic identifiers for 90 days before beginning the process of removing them from live and backup systems.
  • Privacy & Deletion Requests: For privacy or deletion requests, contact hamzaarbi133@gmail.com. We assess requests and, where reasonably possible, delete information we control or use the relevant provider’s deletion tools. We may need information sufficient to locate the records. Your email address alone may not identify your analytics or diagnostic records.
  • Support Correspondence: Support correspondence and feedback emails received at hamzaarbi133@gmail.com are deleted within 90 days after the issue is resolved, unless longer retention is required for legal obligations or an ongoing dispute.

10. Target Audience & Children's Privacy

Quick Panel is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.
